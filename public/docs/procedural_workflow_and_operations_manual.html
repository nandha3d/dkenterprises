3.2 Daily Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1
Morning Shift Opening & Float Entry (07:00 AM)
Head cashier logs in at /cafe, counts the physical opening cash drawer float (e.g. ₹1,000 in loose change), and initializes the drawer shift record.
2
Kitchen Raw Ingredients Issue (07:30 AM)
Kitchen supervisor issues daily raw materials via POST /cafe/raw-materials (Fresh Milk ING-MILK, Cane Sugar ING-SUGAR). ERP executes InventoryMovementService::issue to decrement warehouse inventory via FIFO.
3
Touch POS Terminal Billing (All Day)
Counter staff billing on /cafe/pos. Filter Kaapi (₹35), Masala Chai (₹30), Croissants (₹85), Sourdough (₹120), Muffins (₹65). Receipts post directly to Cash/UPI ledger (1010) and Sales Revenue (4010).
4
Evening Cash Drawer Reconciliation (10:30 PM)
Cashier enters physical cash counted and UPI settlement total. The system calculates:
Discrepancy = (Physical Cash - Opening Float + UPI) - (System Cash + System UPI).
Variance within ±₹10.00 is certified balanced. Shortages are immediately locked and flagged for manager audit.

Every delivery stop dynamically recalibrates the customer's held bottle debt in water_can_inventories. 7. Comprehensive Operational Fraud & Risk Matrix

Formal attack taxonomy, detection algorithms, and automated system responses.

Enterprise Fraud Attack Vector Modus Operandi Detection Algorithm Enforced System Mitigation
Aran Cafe Cash Skimming Cashier collects cash from customers, fails to ring up receipt, or pockets evening float. auditCashDrawer() Drawer status set to short; discrepancy alert dispatched to manager; drawer locked.
Aran Cafe Phantom Kitchen Wastage Chef logs 35L milk spoiled/discarded to conceal inventory taken home. auditIngredientConsumption() Flags IMPOSSIBLE_CONSUMPTION if exceeding stock; recipe variance flagged above 15% tolerance.
Aran Cafe Cross-Branch Stock Hijacking Express counter staff billing inventory belonging exclusively to Main Kitchen warehouse. Tenant Scope Filter Warehouse branch ID must match cashier active session branch; blocked with 403 Forbidden.
TMWS Tankers Odometer Rollback Driver rolls back mechanical odometer to hide unauthorized personal trips. auditTankerOdometer() Asserts End KM >= Start KM; rollback raises critical audit exception.
TMWS Tankers Inflated Distance Billing Driver claims 110 KM for delivery to charge excess KM, while actual route was 32 KM. auditTankerOdometer() Cross-references claimed KM against GPS telematics trace; clamped to verified GPS distance.
TMWS Tankers Water Theft & Decanting Driver leaves with 20KL, delivers 14KL to customer, and sells remaining 6KL for cash. auditWaterDecanting() Calculates Loaded - (Delivered + Returned); shrinkage > 0.3KL triggers water theft charge on driver account.
TMWS Tankers Fake Pumping Delay Driver claims 180 mins motor pumping (₹1,800 charge) for an unloading process taking 25 mins. auditSurchargePumping() Caps allowable pumping time to Delivered KL * 5.0 mins/KL; excess minutes blocked.
DK Waters Driver Can Siphoning Driver takes 80 cans, delivers 65, returns 5, and claims 10 were "lost", reselling them on street. auditRouteCanBalance() Asserts Loaded == Delivered + Returned Filled; missing cans billed as driver shrinkage liability.
DK Waters Phantom Deposit Refund Customer or driver requests cash refund for 20 cans, while customer only holds 4 on record. auditDepositRefundEligibility() Validates Requested <= Customer Held Cans; caps refund strictly to verified cans, blocks fraudulent cash release.
